## Task

Five product teams drew the same components differently. They needed one shared system in Figma and in code.

<Media src="/work/ink-ui/01.svg" alt="" full />

## Research

We ran 12 in-depth interviews and went through funnel analytics and the customer journey map. Key insight: people get lost not because of the number of steps, but because they cannot tell what happens next.

<MediaGrid cols={2}>
  <Media src="/work/ink-ui/02.svg" alt="" />
  <Media src="/work/ink-ui/03.svg" alt="" />
</MediaGrid>

<Quote author="Usability test participant">Finally I understand what happens after I press the button.</Quote>

## Solution

We shaped three hypotheses, tested them on clickable prototypes and picked the one that shortened the path and removed anxiety. Then came iterations with engineering and an A/B test.
